# Changelog

All notable changes to `physketch` are listed here.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Russian documentation, `physketch-doc-ru.pdf`.

### Changed
- Documentation: the forces in the examples for pistons and pulleys and in
  the gallery are drawn with the style `force`.

### Removed
- The undocumented spring keys `zigzag`, `snake` and `shape`; springs are
  always drawn with smooth coils.

## [0.1.0] – 2026-10-20

First public release.
