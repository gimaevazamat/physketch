# Changelog

All notable changes to `physketch` are listed here.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Black-and-white materials for printing: `bw water`, `bw thin water`
  (water over bodies), `bw ice`, `bw snow` and `bw gas`.
- The material `snow` and the colour `snow`.
- `\physketchset{materials=bw}` switches `water`, `ice`, `snow` and `gas` to
  their black-and-white versions; `materials=color` switches back.

### Changed
- The colour `oil` is a paler yellow (`FFD45C` instead of `FFC233`).

## [0.1.1] – 2026-10-29

### Added
- Russian documentation, `physketch-doc-ru.pdf`.

### Changed
- Documentation: the forces in the examples for pistons and pulleys and in
  the gallery are drawn with the style `force`.

### Removed
- The undocumented spring keys `zigzag`, `snake` and `shape`; springs are
  always drawn with smooth coils.

## [0.1.0] – 2026-10-20

First public release.
